Emburse is an expense management and accounts payable automation platform serving over 4 million finance leaders and business travelers worldwide. The company offers REST APIs across its product lines including Emburse Professional (formerly Certify), Emburse Spend (formerly Abacus), and Emburse Enterprise (Chrome River), enabling integrations for expense reports, corporate card management, invoice processing, receipt capture, and reimbursement workflows. The Virtual Cards API provides OAuth2-secured endpoints for creating and managing virtual and physical cards, transactions, members, and accounting data. Emburse Enterprise exposes Swagger-documented APIs for Expense, Invoice, Person, PreApproval, Purchase Order, Vendor, and Allocation resources. API access for the Spend product is available to Partners and customers on the Plus plan or higher.

**Areas:** Expense Management · AP Automation · Corporate Cards · Virtual Cards · Receipt Capture · Reimbursement · Invoice Processing · Finance · Fintech · Ai Apps · Travel And Expense · Invoice Management

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Emburse: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
