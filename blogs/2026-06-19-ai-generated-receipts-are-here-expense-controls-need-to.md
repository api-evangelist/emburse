AI-generated receipts are creating a new category of expense risk, with Emburse research finding that 34% of surveyed U.S. and UK business professionals admit to using AI to generate a fake receipt for a business expense. The article covers how realistic false receipts, financial pressure to submit personal purchases as business expenses, and governance concerns around employer-funded AI tools all strain existing expense controls.
